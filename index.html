A new client project is due to start on Thursday. The signed scope is in place, but the handover record has no confirmed delivery lead. You haven’t asked for a status report.

Recommended next step: ask the operations lead to confirm the assignment and update the handover record.

2. The next action is ready.

Prepared internal message To: Operations lead

“Can you confirm who is leading Thursday’s kickoff and add them to the handover record? The scope is signed; the lead assignment is the remaining item to confirm.”

You can review the source, adjust the message or approve the proposed action.

3. The follow-through stays open until it is checked.

Example action held. The handover requirement remains open.

In this example, the operations lead replies, and the handover record is updated. The agent checks the assignment against the reply and marks that handover requirement confirmed.

If the reply or record is missing, the issue remains open. A sent message alone does not establish that the handover is ready.

  • Operations leadNo reply yet.
  • Handover checklistStill no named delivery lead.
Confirmation is still missing. The requirement remains open.

At the agreed checkpoint, it is surfaced with the latest evidence and a proposed follow-up.

  • Operations leadReply names the delivery lead.
  • Handover checklistNames the same person.
Delivery lead confirmed. The example reply and record agree.

That confirms this requirement. It does not claim the kickoff has happened or the entire project is complete.

04

Keep the decisions that need you.

The right level of control depends on the work. During setup, we would agree which actions can proceed under standing instructions and which need a decision.

  1. Prepare

    Bring together the context and draft the next step.

  2. Proceed within agreed rules

    Carry out defined routine work within its permission limits.

  3. Ask

    Bring you the exact action when it needs your judgment or exceeds those limits.

The goal is fewer things to watch and a clearer view of the things that need your attention.

08

A few questions you may already have.

Do I need to have an AI agent already?

No. The proposed service includes identifying the right work, configuring the agent, connecting the agreed information sources and managing it over time.

Is this only for real estate businesses?

No. Our initial design-partner work is in real estate, but the operating problem extends across businesses with customers, projects, handovers and commitments. Fit depends on the work and access available. Industry-specific coverage needs to be assessed.

Will I have to approve everything?

The aim is to agree standing instructions for suitable routine actions and bring you decisions where your judgment is needed. A pilot should establish that boundary and measure the effort it asks of you.

Can I use it today?

We are developing the service and validating how it works in practice. A conversation is the starting point for assessing fit, scope and readiness for a supervised pilot. It is not an instant software signup.

What are you still having to chase?

Bring one recurring example. We can use it to explore the broader operating burden, where an agent might help and what would need to be proven.
